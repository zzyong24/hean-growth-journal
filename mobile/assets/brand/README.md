# 禾安成长日记
Built-in imagegen generated the project assets; originals retained under Codex generated_images. No user baby photos are used to train or generate these assets.

- hean-wordmark.png: exact six-character horizontal Chinese wordmark 禾安成长日记, custom Song/calligraphic lettering, rice sprout over 禾, forest sage green, transparent background, no extra text.
- report-sage.png: portrait 2:3, sage handmade rice paper, upper 60% empty, delicate watercolor swaddled sleeping baby and rice ears at lower right, botanical edge, no text/UI.
- report-apricot.png: portrait 2:3, ivory rice paper and apricot wash, upper 60% empty, crescent mobile/clouds/sleeping teddy and botanicals at lower right, no text/UI.

These are design backgrounds, not portraits of the actual baby. At runtime a real photo from the report period takes priority; photo composition is local, with crop and fade only. Text/data remain deterministic. Do not rename storage keys, bundle identifiers or URL scheme during this visual rebrand.
